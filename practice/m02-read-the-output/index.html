Why the order is fixed in Part A

Here the order is fixed so everyone's numbers match the sample. Each order was chosen by trying a small grid of orders on the training rows only and keeping the lowest AIC. The test months played no part in the choice. d = 1 because the ADF test in A1 said the series needs differencing. D = 1 because the series repeats a strong seasonal pattern (see the month averages and the decomposition in A1), and a seasonal difference removes it. In B1 you can compare this order with what auto_arima picks.

Look for

  • RMSE in thousand passengersindex points, the units you set in A1.
  • R² compares your forecast with a lazy one that says every test month equals the test-year average. Close to 1 means the model explains most of the test year's ups and downs. Below 0 would mean it did worse than the lazy forecast.
  • The misses: actual minus forecast, so positive means the forecast was too low. Are the signs mixed or mostly the same?
